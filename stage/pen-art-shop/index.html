Details

Shipping, printing & contact

Shipping

Originals ship flat and protected within the United States, and U.S. shipping is included in the $25 price. Inventory and fulfilment are handled by hand, so allow a little time for dispatch. International shipping isn’t offered yet.

Which original you receive

You buy a series, not a specific numbered drawing. Fulfilment picks one of that series’ remaining originals and ships it. Every original is a unique hand-plotted variation of the series and will differ from the preview image. There is no way to request a particular one.

Printing the subscriber image at home

The subscriber scan is sized for a 4×6 print but prints cleanly larger. Any home printer or local print shop works. The file is for your personal use only — please don’t share, redistribute, or sell it or prints of it.

Payment

Checkout, card payment, receipts, and the shipping address you enter are all handled on Stripe’s hosted pages. This site has no cart, no accounts, and no customer database.
