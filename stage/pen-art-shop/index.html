Why every original is different

One piece I keep, 25 originals for you

How a series is made
Every series starts as code I write. It generates the design, and I ink the results on a pen plotter — nothing is sketched or drawn freehand. The very first piece off the plotter is the one I scan at high resolution and keep for my own records; that scan is the free printable I email to subscribers, and the image on this page is only a small, low-resolution version of it.
The 25 originals you can buy
I plot 25 originals for each series, one at a time. The generative code and the plotting process make every one slightly different, so no two share the same lines. You choose the series; I ship one of its originals — a variation of the design shown, not a copy of it, a piece that exists only once. When all 25 have found homes, the series is marked Sold out.

Shipping, printing & contact

Shipping

Originals ship flat and protected anywhere in the United States, and shipping is included in the $25 price. I pack and mail every order by hand, so allow a little time for dispatch. International shipping isn’t offered yet.

Which original you receive

You buy a series, not a specific piece. I pick one of that series’ remaining originals and ship it — a unique pen-plotted variation that will differ from the image shown. There’s no way to request a particular one.

Printing the subscriber file at home

The file is sized for a 4×6 print but holds up larger. Any home printer or local print shop works. It’s for your personal use only — please don’t share, redistribute, or sell it or prints of it.
