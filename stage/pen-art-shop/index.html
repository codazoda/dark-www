How a series works

26 variants, three roles

1 prototype — kept, never sold
The first plot of a new design. Once it looks right I keep it in my own collection and scan it at high resolution as the series’ “gold master.”
1 subscriber print — free, digital only
The printable scan derived from that prototype. Mailing-list subscribers get it for every series to print at home. It is related to the series but is not a copy of any original for sale.
25 originals — physical, $25 each
Drawn individually on the plotter to finish the edition. Each is numbered (for example Series 01 · 07 / 25). After all 25 are produced and checked, the generative source and production files are deleted, so the physical edition can never be re-made.

Details

Shipping, printing & contact

Shipping

Originals ship flat and protected within the United States, and U.S. shipping is included in the $25 price. Inventory and fulfilment are handled by hand, so allow a little time for dispatch. International shipping isn’t offered yet.

Printing the subscriber image at home

The subscriber scan is sized for a 4×6 print but prints cleanly larger. Any home printer or local print shop works. The file is for your personal use only — please don’t share, redistribute, or sell it or prints of it.

Payment

Checkout, card payment, receipts, and the shipping address you enter are all handled on Stripe’s hosted pages. This site has no cart, no accounts, and no customer database.

Privacy

No advertising pixels, no behavioural analytics, no cookies, no visitor identifiers on this site. Email addresses live only with SendFox; payment and address details live only with Stripe.
